The Problem We're Solving
Traditional bootcamps struggle with students who attend but don't actually learn, tutors waiting for stragglers, and employers who can't verify real skills. SkillPays creates a blockchain-based "learn and earn" system where students must prove mastery through challenges to unlock badges, NFTs, and tokens.

Our Solution
SkillPays creates a competitive learning ecosystem where knowledge directly translates to earnings and career opportunities. This means bootcamps get transparent progress tracking, students earn verifiable credentials with the bootcamp’s fixed time, and employers can trust the skills are real. No more fake completions, wasted time, or unverified certificates just proof-based learning that rewards genuine achievement.

The Vision
SkillPays isn't just another learning platform. It's a platform focused on originality, producing students who acquire actual ready-for-market skills, not just abstract knowledge.
The platform introduces leaderboards and hackathons for top students, allowing them to showcase their skills by building solutions and innovations to compete for a prize.
Our platform also allows organizations to create and monetize bootcamps (optional). This makes it a win-win for everybody.
